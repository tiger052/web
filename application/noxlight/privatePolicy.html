Nox Light Privacy Policy

Last updated: Effective from app launch (v1.0)

Nox Light ("we", "our", or "us"), operated by Jae1 Soft, provides the Nox Light mobile application (the "App").

Information We Do Not Collect

Nox Light does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information from users. The App operates entirely on your device without sending data to our servers.

Third-Party Advertising

We use Google AdMob to display advertisements within the App. AdMob may collect and use data to serve personalized ads, including:

  • Device advertising ID
  • IP address
  • General location (country/region)

For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy.

iOS Users: You will be asked for permission to track your activity across apps for personalized advertising. You may decline this permission; the App will continue to function normally with non-personalized ads.

Camera Permission

The App requests camera permission solely to control the device's camera flash (torch). No photos or videos are captured, stored, or transmitted.

Wake Lock

The App uses a wake lock permission to prevent the screen from turning off while the flashlight is in use. No data is collected through this permission.

Children's Privacy

Nox Light is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted within the App or on our store listing.
