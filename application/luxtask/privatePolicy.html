LuxTask Privacy Policy

Last updated: Effective from app launch (v1.0)

English 한국어

LuxTask ("we", "our", or "us"), operated by Jae1 Soft, provides the LuxTask mobile to-do application (the "App").

Local Data Storage

All data you enter in the App — including tasks, notes, and checklists — is stored solely on your device. We do not transmit or collect this data on our servers.

Notification Permission

The App requests notification permission to send due-date reminders. Notifications are processed entirely on-device, and no personal information is collected through this permission.

Microphone Permission (Voice Input)

The App requests microphone permission solely to enable voice-to-text input for tasks, notes, and checklist items. Recognized speech is processed by the device's built-in speech recognition engine (Android: Google, iOS: Apple). Jae1 Soft does not collect, store, or transmit any voice data.

Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

We use Google AdMob to display advertisements within the App. AdMob may collect and use data to serve personalized ads, including:

  • Device advertising ID
  • IP address
  • General location (country/region)

For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy.

iOS Users: You will be asked for permission to track your activity across apps for personalized advertising. You may decline this permission; the App will continue to function normally with non-personalized ads.

Firebase (Analytics · Crashlytics · Remote Config)

The App uses the following Google Firebase services to improve quality and stability:

  • Firebase Analytics: Collects anonymous usage statistics to help us understand how features are used.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: Automatically collects and reports crash data to help us fix bugs.
  • Firebase Remote Config: Allows us to adjust app settings (such as ad behavior) remotely without an app update.

Data collected by Firebase is processed in a non-personally-identifiable form. For more information, see the Firebase Privacy Policy.

Children's Privacy

LuxTask is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted within the App or on our store listing.
